Frequently Asked

Everything you're probably wondering

Is the report really free?
Yes. Your Ski Demand Report is completely free with no obligation. You'll see your Weather-Marketing Readiness Score, targeted skiing households, recommended plan and budget, and you can download the plan as a PDF.
How does weather-triggered advertising work?
We identify the weather moments that drive ski demand in your market — powder days, cold & snowmaking windows, bluebird weekends, holiday forecasts — and activate your ads to skiing households exactly when those conditions hit, pausing on rain, warm, or high-wind days.
Who will actually see my ads?
Targeted skiing households in your feeder markets, layered with competitor-resort, ski-shop & travel-corridor geotargeting and location look-back, across Connected TV, streaming radio, podcasts, data-driven display, and digital out-of-home.
How is my budget spent?
Your plan concentrates spend in historically strong snow windows and steps it down in weak-weather weeks — and the report shows how much budget that could protect. Delivery estimates use planning assumptions and vary by market, inventory, and dates.
How fast can we launch?
Most resorts can launch within days. Start with the free report, and a Smart 1 strategist can review it and build your campaign from there.
